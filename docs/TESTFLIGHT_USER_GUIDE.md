# Dysarthria App: TestFlight Beta Installation Guide

Welcome to the beta testing program for the Dysarthria App! This guide will walk you through installing the app on your Apple device (iPhone or iPad) using Apple's official beta testing app, **TestFlight**.

---

## Step 1: Install the TestFlight App
Before you can download the Dysarthria App, you must install Apple's TestFlight app on your device.

1. On your iPhone or iPad, open the **App Store**.
2. Search for **TestFlight**.
3. Tap **Get** or the cloud icon to install it (it is free).

---

## Step 2: Accept Your Invitation
Once you are added as a tester, you will receive an invitation email from Apple on behalf of the developer.

1. Open the email inbox on your iOS device.
2. Locate the email with the subject: **"Apple: TestFlight: You're invited to test [App Name]"** or **"[Developer Name] has invited you to test 'Dysarthria App'"**.
3. Open the email and tap the blue **View in TestFlight** or **Start Testing** button.

---

## Step 3: Redeem and Install the App

Depending on your device setup, one of the following two scenarios will occur:

### Scenario A: Automatic Redirect (Recommended)
If you tap the link on your iOS device with TestFlight already installed:
1. The **TestFlight** app will automatically open.
2. Tap **Accept** to accept the invitation.
3. Tap **Install** to download and install the Dysarthria App.

### Scenario B: Manual Redeem Code
If TestFlight does not open automatically, or if you are viewing the email on a different device:
1. The web page that opens will display an **8-character activation code**. Copy this code.
2. Open the **TestFlight** app on your iPhone/iPad.
3. Tap **Redeem** in the top-right corner.
4. Paste or type the 8-character activation code, then tap **Redeem**.
5. Tap **Install** to download the Dysarthria App.

---

## Step 4: Open and Test the App
Once the installation is complete:
* You can open the app directly from **TestFlight** by tapping **Open**.
* Alternatively, look for the **Dysarthria App** on your Home Screen. It will have a **small orange dot** next to its name, indicating it is a TestFlight beta version.

---

## How to Get Updates
When a new version of the app is available:
* You will receive a push notification from TestFlight (if enabled) and an email.
* Open the **TestFlight** app and tap **Update** next to the Dysarthria App.
* **Auto-Update**: We recommend enabling **Automatic Updates** inside the TestFlight app (tap the Dysarthria App in your TestFlight app list, and toggle **Automatic Updates** on) to automatically receive the latest fixes.
