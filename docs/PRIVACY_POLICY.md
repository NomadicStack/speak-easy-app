# Privacy Policy for SpeakEasy

**Effective Date: June 11, 2026**

SpeakEasy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the SpeakEasy mobile application on iPadOS.

## 1. No Data Collection
SpeakEasy is designed with privacy as a core principle. 
* **Audio Recordings:** We do not collect, store, or transmit your voice or audio recordings. All voice recording and speech-to-text transcription are performed locally, offline, and in real-time on your iPad device.
* **Transcribed Text:** The text generated from your speech remains strictly on your device. It is never uploaded to any remote servers, nor is it shared with any third parties.
* **Personal Information:** The application does not collect any personal identifying information (PII), device identifiers, usage statistics, or analytics.

## 2. On-Device Processing
The app utilizes local on-device machine learning models (such as WhisperKit and Gemma) to transcribe speech. After entering a valid activation token, the model files are downloaded directly from our secure backend to your device. Once downloaded, the application operates 100% offline, and no network traffic is generated for processing your speech.
