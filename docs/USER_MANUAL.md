# User Manual: SpeakEasy

SpeakEasy is an accessible communication app designed for people with dysarthria. It uses on-device AI to transcribe speech and expand short phrases into full, natural sentences.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Transcription (Basic Voice-to-Text)
- **What it is:** Converts your spoken words into text on the screen.
- **How to use:** 
  1. Open the **Transcribe** tab.
  2. Tap the large **Blue Microphone**.
  3. Speak clearly. Tap the **Stop** button when finished.
  4. Your text will appear in large, easy-to-read font.
- **Options:** You can **Copy** the text or **Share** it using the icons above the text area.

### Smart Speak (AI Sentences)
- **What it is:** Take a short phrase (shorthand) and turn it into 3 natural sentences.
- **How to use:**
  1. Tap the **Smart Speak** tab.
  2. If it's your first time, follow the prompts to download the **AI Brain** (Gemma 4).
  3. Speak a short phrase (e.g., "thirsty water").
  4. The AI will generate 3 options (e.g., "Could I please have some water?").
  5. **Listen:** Tap a sentence or the **Purple Speaker** icon to hear it read aloud.
  6. **Message:** Tap the **Blue Message** icon to send that sentence as a text.

---

## 2. Managing Contacts

You can save common contacts (like Mom, Dad, or a Caregiver) to send messages even faster.

1. In the **Smart Speak** tab, tap the **Purple Brain Icon** in the top right.
2. Under **Contacts**, you can:
   - Set a **Primary Caregiver** (the main person you message).
   - Add **Other Contacts** with a Name and Number.
3. **Smart Routing:** If you speak a contact's name in your shorthand (e.g., "Dad thirsty"), the **Message** button will automatically address the text to **Dad**.

---

## 3. Settings & AI Brain

Tapping the **Purple Brain Icon** in the Smart Speak tab opens your settings:

- **AI Brain (Models):** View and download the Gemma 4 model (2.6GB). This is required for Smart Speak to work without internet.
- **Simulate AI:** If you are testing the app or have low storage, you can enable **Simulate AI Model** under "Advanced". This provides "fake" sentences for testing purposes.
- **Clear Cache:** If you need to free up space, use **Clear Cache** to delete downloaded models.

---

## 4. Accessibility Tips

- **Large Targets:** Every button in SpeakEasy is oversized. You can tap anywhere on a sentence card to speak it aloud.
- **Navigation:** The tabs at the bottom are extra large. You don't need to be precise—just tap the general area of the icon or text.
- **Automatic:** In Smart Speak, the AI expansion starts automatically as soon as you stop speaking. You don't need to press an extra "Expand" button.
